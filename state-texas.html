Two Texas Questions to Settle Early

Can I submit a boat stored near Galveston Bay or Clear Lake?

You may submit it for individual review. A boat near Galveston Bay may involve different questions than one near Lake Travis or Possum Kingdom Lake. Give the exact marina, yard, mooring, residence, or storage address; describe whether the boat is afloat, blocked, lifted, or trailered; and disclose access limits, balances, liens, and facility deadlines.

Which Texas ownership records should I gather?

Texas owners should gather title and registration records for the boat and motor where applicable, trailer title or registration, lien releases, Coast Guard documentation, and marina or storage details. Do not sign a title, cancel storage or insurance, or release the boat on the strength of an inquiry alone.
