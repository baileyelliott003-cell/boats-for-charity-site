Lake Travis Elevation Swings, Low-Water Ramps, and Lake Austin Slips

Lake Travis is a flood-control reservoir managed by the Lower Colorado River Authority (LCRA). Its elevation can fluctuate by more than 50 vertical feet between wet and severe drought years. During low-water periods, floating docks must be repeatedly pushed further into deep channels, and public boat ramps frequently close due to exposed limestone bedrock.

For boats berthed in Lakeway, Point Venture, or Hudson Bend, monthly slip rents ($300 to $900/mo), lift maintenance, and sun-damaged vinyl upkeep continue regardless of whether lake levels allow easy boating. When you are ready to transition away from boat ownership, donating through Boats for Charity (501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 41-2487552) allows you to end these expenses immediately.

Operational Transparency: How Our Process Works

Our guided donation framework guarantees complete transparency for Central Texas donors:

  • The boat remains in your possession: You do not need to transport the vessel to an auction lot. It stays securely in your Austin marina slip, driveway, or private dock while our team manages the review.
  • Zero transportation costs for the charity or donor: We do not dispatch commercial haulers. Instead, we list the boat for public charity auction to verified buyers nationwide.
  • Buyer-arranged pickup after cleared funds: After the winning buyer's payment clears, the buyer coordinates trailering and pickup directly with you at their expense.
  • Tax documentation delivery: You receive an official IRS Form 1098-C reporting gross sales proceeds for your federal tax filing.

Texas Parks & Wildlife (TPWD) Titling Rules

Texas law enforces strict dual-titling regulations. The Texas Parks and Wildlife Department (TPWD) titles motorized watercraft and all outboard motors separately. Manufactured trailers are titled through the Texas Department of Motor Vehicles (TxDMV). You will need clear Texas Certificate of Title documents for the hull, outboard motor (if applicable), and trailer, along with any lien satisfaction releases.
