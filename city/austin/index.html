Highland Lakes, Lake Travis, and Drought Level Realities

Austin boating centers on the Colorado River chain of reservoirs. Lake Travis offers deep water boating while Lake Austin features constant-level cruising. However, drought cycles can drop Lake Travis significantly, stranding boat lifts and closing public boat ramps.

When an owner decides it is time to move on from a vessel, private selling during low-water seasons is slow and frustrating. Boats for Charity (501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 41-2487552) reviews powerboats, sailboats, ski boats, and trailers individually with zero owner expense.

Austin Storage & Hill Country Access

Lake Travis Marinas

Lakeway Marina, Hurst Harbor, Emerald Point, and Crosswater Marina: disclose floating dock location, gate access, and electrical status.

Private Lifts & Ramps

Disclose low-water shoreline clearance along Lake Austin or Lake Travis, steep Hill Country driveway slopes, and lift operability.

Trailered Ski & Wake Boats

Document trailer axle condition, tires, lights, coupler, and Texas trailer titling/registration records.

Texas TPWD Title & Outboard Titling

Texas requires title transfer through the Texas Parks and Wildlife Department (TPWD), which titles boat hulls AND outboard motors separately. Inboard ski/wake boats transfer under single-hull title rules. Gather your Texas boat title, trailer paperwork, and lien release forms. See our paperwork checklist and tax deduction guide.
