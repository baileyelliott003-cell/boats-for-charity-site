Sailing Capital of the World, Brenton Cove, and Wintering

Newport Harbor features one of the world's most distinguished fleets of sailing yachts, downeast cruisers, classic wooden craft, and sportfishers. But Rhode Island's harsh freezing winters require complete diesel and freshwater winterization, anti-freeze flushing, and expensive dry dock storage.

When an owner is ready to move on from a vessel, private selling often requires waiting months for spring launch season. Boats for Charity (501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 41-2487552) reviews powerboats, sailboats, and yachts year-round with zero owner expense.
