Boat Donation in Annapolis, Maryland

Renowned as America's sailing capital, Annapolis sits at the mouth of the Severn River on the western shore of Chesapeake Bay. From mooring fields in Spa Creek and marina slips across Back Creek and Eastport to boatyards along the South River and Magothy River, sailors and powerboaters cherish these waters. However, Chesapeake winter freeze risks, seasonal shrink-wrapping requirements, and rising mooring or slip fees can turn an underutilized sailboat, cruiser, or center console into a costly burden. Chesapeake Brackish Salinity, Spa Creek Moorings, and Winterization

The middle Chesapeake Bay environment around Annapolis features brackish water with distinct operational considerations. Brackish water fosters aggressive biofouling and zebra mussel attachment in freshwater tributaries, while winter ice floes on the Severn River require seasonal de-icers (bubblers) or complete winter dry-land haul-out.

For cruising sailboats and trawlers berthed in Back Creek or Eastport, annual costs for winter storage, shrink-wrap, spring bottom paint, and rigging maintenance can exceed $5,000 to $10,000 annually. If your vessel is sitting idle across seasons, donating through Boats for Charity (501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 41-2487552) provides a seamless and cost-free alternative.

Operational Transparency: How Our Process Works

Our guided donation model ensures full clarity for Maryland donors:

  • The boat remains in your possession: You do not need to move the boat to an impound yard. It stays securely in your Annapolis wet slip, mooring field, or yard while our team evaluates and lists the vessel.
  • Zero transportation costs for the charity or donor: We do not dispatch commercial haulers. Instead, we list the boat for public charity auction to verified buyers nationwide.
  • Buyer-arranged pickup after cleared funds: After the winning buyer completes verified payment, the buyer coordinates haul-out, de-rigging, or trailering directly with you at their expense.
  • Tax documentation delivery: You receive an official IRS Form 1098-C reporting gross sales proceeds for your federal tax filing.

Maryland DNR Titling & Paperwork Requirements

Maryland mandates clear title transfer through the Maryland Department of Natural Resources (DNR) Licensing & Registration Service. Motorized watercraft and sailboats over 16 feet require a Maryland Certificate of Title and MD registration numbers. Trailers are titled separately through the Maryland Motor Vehicle Administration (MVA). USCG-documented yachts follow National Vessel Documentation Center rules.
