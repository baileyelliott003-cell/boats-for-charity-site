America's Sailing Capital, Spa Creek, and Mid-Atlantic Seasons

Annapolis has one of the densest concentrations of sailing yachts, coastal cruisers, and downeast powerboats in the United States. From Eastport boatyards along Back Creek to deep-draft slips on the Severn River, local vessels face freezing winters and brackish summer fouling.

When you are ready to move on from a vessel, private selling often requires months of organizing winter showings and paying boatyard storage. Boats for Charity (501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 41-2487552) reviews powerboats, sailboats, yachts, and trailers individually with zero owner fees.

Annapolis Marina Access & Boatyard Logistics

Back Creek & Spa Creek Slips

Disclose mooring ball vs wet slip status, bridge opening schedules (Spa Creek Bridge), and marina electrical balances.

Dry Storage & Boatyards

Provide boatyard name, blocking status, travel-lift haul-out reservations, and vendor access rules.

Trailered Boats

Document trailer axle condition, tires, lights, coupler, and Maryland trailer title/registration status.

Maryland DNR Title & USCG NVDC Documentation

Maryland requires clear title transfer through the Department of Natural Resources (DNR) Licensing & Registration Service. Gather your Maryland Certificate of Title, MD registration decal numbers, trailer title, and any lien satisfaction records. See our paperwork checklist and tax deduction guide.
