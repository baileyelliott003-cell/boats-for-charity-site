Boca Ciega Bay Shallow Flats, Gulf Passes, and Hull Maintenance

St. Petersburg is surrounded by water on three sides: Tampa Bay to the east, Boca Ciega Bay and the Intracoastal Waterway to the west, and the Gulf of Mexico just beyond Bunces Pass and Pass-a-Grille Channel. While this geography provides superb cruising, shallow passes and shifting sandbars subject hulls and outdrives to frequent grounding strain.

Moreover, warm Gulf waters accelerate biofouling, requiring monthly diver bottom cleanings and frequent zinc replacement. When an owner steps away from active boating, maintaining a wet slip or paid high-and-dry bay quickly becomes cost-prohibitive. Requesting a transparent donation review with Boats for Charity (501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 41-2487552) lets you resolve ownership with zero out-of-pocket disposal fees.

Operational Transparency: How Our Process Works

Our guided charitable process is designed for complete donor peace of mind:

  • The boat remains in your possession: You do not need to move the boat to an impound yard. It stays securely in your St. Petersburg marina slip, private dock, or driveway while our team manages the review.
  • Zero transportation costs for the charity or donor: We do not dispatch commercial haulers. Instead, we list the boat for public charity auction to verified buyers nationwide.
  • Buyer-arranged pickup after cleared funds: After the winning buyer completes verified payment, they coordinate directly with you to schedule pickup, managing all trailering, haul-out, or towing.
  • Tax documentation delivery: Upon completion, you receive official IRS Form 1098-C reporting gross sale proceeds for your federal tax filing.

St. Petersburg Marina, Bay & Ramp Access Logistics

When preparing your intake review, please share the specific access characteristics of your moorage:

Municipal & Private Marinas

Share facility name (e.g., St. Petersburg Municipal Marina, Maximo Marina, Harborage Marina, Tierra Verde Marina Resort), slip number, and dockmaster rules.

Boca Ciega & Canal Docks

Disclose low-tide depth, bridge clearances (Pinellas Bayway / Central Ave), seawall condition, and dock lift capacity.

Trailered Powerboats & Sailcraft

Document trailer axle condition, tires, lights, and Florida DHSMV trailer title/registration status.

Florida Title & Paperwork Requirements

Florida law mandates clear legal title documentation. Gather your Florida Certificate of Title (or electronic DHSMV confirmation), Florida vessel registration numbers (FL#), trailer title/registration, and any lien satisfaction forms. Documented vessels follow USCG National Vessel Documentation Center procedures. Review our boat donation paperwork checklist and IRS tax deduction guide.
