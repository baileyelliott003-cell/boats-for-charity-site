Pacific Salt, Marina del Rey Rents, and Coastal Storage

Los Angeles is home to some of the largest small-craft harbors in North America. Marina del Rey houses over 4,600 wet slips alone, while the Port of Los Angeles and San Pedro Bay host deep-draft offshore cruisers and sportfishers. However, constant saltwater exposure, bottom fouling, and high harbor slip rates create heavy carrying costs for owners whose boating habits have slowed.

When an owner decides it is time to move on, private selling in LA often requires months of organizing sea trials and dealing with lowball offers. Boats for Charity (501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 41-2487552) reviews powerboats, sailboats, sportfishers, and trailers individually with zero owner expense.

California watercraft require clean title transfer through the California DMV or the USCG National Vessel Documentation Center (NVDC) for documented yachts.
