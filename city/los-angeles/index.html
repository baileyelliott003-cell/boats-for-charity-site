Marina del Rey Slip Regulations, Pacific Swells, and Holding Costs

Marina del Rey accommodates over 4,600 recreational boats across multiple private anchorages and county-leased docks. Operating a boat along the Southern California coast requires compliance with strict County of Los Angeles Department of Beaches and Harbors ordinances, including mandatory operational engine checks and seaworthiness inspections.

Between monthly wet slip rents ($500 to $1,800/mo depending on LOA and beam), county possessory interest taxes, bi-weekly bottom cleanings, and maintenance on aging inboard or outboard systems, keeping an inactive boat becomes a major financial drain. Requesting a transparent donation review with Boats for Charity (501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 41-2487552) lets you resolve ownership with zero out-of-pocket disposal fees.

Operational Transparency: How Our Process Works

Our guided donation model ensures full clarity for Los Angeles donors:

  • The boat remains in your possession: You do not need to move the vessel to a distant lot. It stays safely at your current marina slip, private yard, or driveway while our team evaluates and lists the vessel.
  • Zero transportation costs for the charity or donor: We do not dispatch commercial haulers. Instead, we list the boat for public charity auction to verified buyers nationwide.
  • Buyer-arranged pickup after cleared funds: Once payment has cleared following the auction, the verified buyer coordinates haul-out and transport directly with you at their expense.
  • Tax documentation delivery: You receive an official IRS Form 1098-C reporting gross sales proceeds for your federal and state tax filings.

California DMV Titling & Registration Paperwork

California requires clear title documentation through the California Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV). Motorized boats and sailboats over 8 feet carry a Certificate of Ownership (California Pink Slip) and CF numbers. Trailers carry separate DMV titles or permanent trailer identification (PTI) registrations. USCG-documented yachts are handled through the National Vessel Documentation Center.
