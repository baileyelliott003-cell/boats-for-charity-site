Clear Lake Marinas, Galveston Bay Salt, and Storm Preparedness

The Clear Lake basin hosts thousands of wet slips and dry-stack storage bays across Kemah, Seabrook, and Nassau Bay. While direct access through the Clear Creek Channel into Galveston Bay is ideal for weekend anglers and cruisers, the region's subtropical weather accelerates wear on raw-water cooling systems, vinyl seating, and marine canvas.

Furthermore, hurricane season demands active haul-out contracts and named-storm insurance deductibles. When owners stop using their watercraft, monthly slip rentals and seasonal maintenance bills continue to accrue. Operational Transparency: How Our Process Works

We pride ourselves on clear, ethical communication throughout the donation cycle:

  • The boat remains in your possession: You do not need to move the boat. It stays securely in your Houston marina slip, driveway, or storage lot while our team manages the review and listing.
  • Zero transportation costs for the charity or donor: We do not dispatch commercial haulers. Instead, we list the boat for public charity auction to verified buyers nationwide.

Texas Parks & Wildlife (TPWD) Titling Rules

Texas law enforces strict dual-titling regulations for boats. The Texas Parks and Wildlife Department (TPWD) titles motorized watercraft and all outboard motors separately. Manufactured trailers are titled through the Texas Department of Motor Vehicles (TxDMV). You will need clear Texas Certificate of Title documents for the hull, outboard motor (if applicable), and trailer, along with any lien releases.
