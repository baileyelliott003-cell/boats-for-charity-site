Lake Michigan Harbors, Chicago River, and Winter Haul-Outs

Chicago boasts one of the largest municipal harbor systems in North America with over 6,000 slips and moorings. The fleet spans sailing yachts, express cruisers, center consoles, and runabouts. However, harsh Midwestern freezes require complete engine antifreeze winterization, water system draining, and strict October haul-out deadlines.

When an owner is ready to move on, private selling in late fall or winter is notoriously difficult. Boats for Charity (501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 41-2487552) reviews powerboats, sailboats, and trailers year-round with zero owner expense.

Illinois IDNR Title & Registration

Watercraft in Illinois are titled and registered through the Illinois Department of Natural Resources (IDNR), while boat trailers are titled through the Illinois Secretary of State (SOS). Gather your Illinois Certificate of Title, IL registration card, trailer title, and lien release forms. See our paperwork checklist and tax deduction guide.
