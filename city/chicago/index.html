Lake Michigan Harbors, Winter Freeze Cycles, and Chicago Park District Rules

Chicago boasts one of the largest municipal harbor systems in the world, managed by Westrec on behalf of the Chicago Park District. Every October 31st, all ten harbors officially close for the winter. Boat owners face mandatory haul-out deadlines to prevent ice damage and hull crushing from thick Lake Michigan ice sheets.

Between annual marina slip fees, crane travel-lift haul-out fees, winter shrink-wrapping, indoor heated or outdoor cradle storage, and spring commissioning, holding an unused sailboat or cruiser frequently costs $4,000 to $9,000 every single year. When family schedules change or a vessel sits unused across multiple seasons, donating your boat through Boats for Charity (501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 41-2487552) lets you resolve ownership with zero out-of-pocket disposal fees.

Operational Transparency: How Our Process Works

Our guided donation model guarantees complete transparency for every Illinois donor:

  • The boat remains in your possession: You do not need to move the boat to an impound yard. It stays securely in your Chicago harbor slip, winter yard, or driveway while our team evaluates and lists the vessel.
  • Zero transportation costs for the charity or donor: We do not dispatch commercial haulers. Instead, we list the boat for public charity auction to verified buyers nationwide.
  • Buyer-arranged pickup after cleared funds: After the winning buyer's payment clears, the buyer coordinates haul-out, ramp loading, or flatbed transport directly with you at their expense.
  • Tax documentation delivery: You receive an official IRS Form 1098-C reporting gross sales proceeds for your federal tax filing.

Trailered Powerboats

Document trailer axle condition, tires, surge brakes, lights, and Illinois SOS trailer title/registration status.

Illinois DNR Titling & Paperwork Requirements

Illinois requires clear documentation through the Illinois Department of Natural Resources (IDNR) Watercraft Division. Motorized watercraft and sailboats over 12 feet must carry an IDNR Certificate of Title and IL registration numbers. Trailers are titled through the Illinois Secretary of State (SOS). Documented vessels follow USCG National Vessel Documentation Center rules. Review our boat donation paperwork checklist and IRS tax deduction guide.
