Biscayne Bay, Miami River, and the Dry-Stack Habit

Miami is an elite boating market: Biscayne Bay stretching south past Key Biscayne, the bustling Miami River marina corridor, and the Atlantic just beyond Government Cut. With waterfront space at an extreme premium, a large share of local center consoles, cruisers, and sportfishers live in dry-stack rack facilities.

It is remarkably convenient right up until outings slow down — and suddenly you are paying thousands annually to store a vessel you rarely use. Requesting an individual donation review with Boats for Charity (501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 41-2487552) lets you halt those holding costs immediately.

Year-Round Salt, Tropical UV, and Hurricane Preparation

South Florida brings unique vessel considerations: intense UV degrading upholstery and gelcoat, saltwater corrosion on running gear and outboards, and mandatory hurricane haul-out plans. Even covered rack storage vessels can suffer from stale ethanol fuel and drained batteries. We review vessels in running or non-running condition with candor and care.

Miami Marina & Facility Access

Dry-Stack Racks & Marinas

Provide facility name, rack bay number, forklift launch requirements, gate access codes, and account status.

Trailered Vessels

Document trailer axle condition, tires, lights, coupler, and separate trailer registration or title.

Wet Slips & Canal Docks

Disclose draft, beam, canal bridge clearances, tide restrictions, and dockmaster vendor access rules.

Florida Title & Paperwork Requirements

Florida requires clear documentation for vessel transfers. Gather your Florida title (or electronic title confirmation), Florida registration numbers (FL#), trailer title, and any lien satisfactions. Documented yachts go through the USCG National Vessel Documentation Center. Review our boat donation paperwork checklist and tax deduction guide.
