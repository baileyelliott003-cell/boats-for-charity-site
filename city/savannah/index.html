Georgia Salt Marshes, 9-Foot Tidal Swings, and Thunderbolt Boatyards

Coastal Georgia experiences some of the most dramatic tidal swings on the Atlantic seaboard, frequently exceeding 8 to 9 feet between high and low water. In winding tidal creeks across Chatham County, vessels left on private docks or shallow slips can find themselves grounded on pluff mud twice daily if low water depths are miscalculated.

For vessels berthed in Thunderbolt or Isle of Hope, monthly slip rent, dock electric, hull diving, and seasonal storm preparations represent an ongoing financial liability. When personal priorities shift, donating your boat through Boats for Charity (501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 41-2487552) lets you resolve ownership with zero out-of-pocket disposal fees.

Operational Transparency: How Our Process Works

Our guided donation model ensures full clarity for Georgia donors:

  • The boat remains in your possession: You do not need to transport the vessel to an auction yard. It remains securely in your Savannah marina slip, driveway, or private dock while our team manages the review.
  • Zero transportation costs for the charity or donor: We do not dispatch commercial haulers. Instead, we list the boat for public charity auction to verified buyers nationwide.
  • Buyer-arranged pickup after cleared funds: Once payment has cleared following the auction, the verified buyer coordinates haul-out and transport directly with you at their expense.
  • Tax documentation delivery: You receive an official IRS Form 1098-C reporting gross sales proceeds for your federal tax filing.

Georgia DNR Titling & Paperwork Requirements

Georgia requires clear title and registration documentation through the Georgia Department of Natural Resources (DNR) Wildlife Resources Division. Georgia issues boat registrations (GA numbers) and electronic vessel titles for vessels purchased or transferred after state titling laws took effect. Trailers are registered through county tax commissioner tag offices. Review our boat donation paperwork checklist and IRS tax deduction guide.
