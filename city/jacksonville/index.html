Boat Donation in Jacksonville, Florida

Spanning the broad St. Johns River, the Ortega River yachting cluster, the Intracoastal Waterway, and Mayport naval inlets, Jacksonville is Northeast Florida's recreational boating center. From sportfisher berths in Jacksonville Beach to cruiser slips in downtown riverfront marinas and dry-lot storage across Arlington and Mandarin, watercraft ownership requires active stewardship. However, river currents, brackish barnacle growth, and ongoing dockage fees can make maintaining an unused vessel a continuous financial drain. St. Johns River Currents, Ortega River Boatyards, and Brackish Water

The St. Johns River is one of the few northward-flowing rivers in North America, with heavy tidal influence extending over 100 miles south of the Atlantic ocean inlet. The Ortega River basin is a historic hub for wooden boat restoration, classic trawlers, and offshore cruising yachts. However, fluctuating freshwater-saltwater transitions produce rapid biofouling, demanding frequent hull diving and bottom maintenance.

When an owner retires from active boating, wet slip fees, dock lift upkeep, and insurance premiums continue to accumulate. Requesting an individual donation review with Boats for Charity (501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 41-2487552) allows you to resolve vessel custody with zero out-of-pocket disposal fees.

Operational Transparency: How Our Process Works

Our guided charitable process is designed for complete donor peace of mind:

  • The boat remains in your possession: You do not have to move the vessel to a commercial yard. It remains safely in your Jacksonville wet slip, private dock, or driveway while our team evaluates and lists the vessel.
  • Zero transportation costs for the charity or donor: We do not dispatch commercial haulers. Instead, our team markets the vessel to verified buyers through public charitable auctions.
  • Buyer-arranged pickup after cleared funds: After the winning buyer completes verified payment, they coordinate directly with you to schedule pickup, managing all trailering, haul-out, or towing.
  • Tax documentation delivery: Upon completion, you receive official IRS Form 1098-C reporting gross sale proceeds for your federal tax filing.

Jacksonville Marina, River & Ramp Access Logistics

When preparing your intake review, please share the specific access characteristics of your moorage:

River Marinas & Boatyards

Share facility name (e.g., Ortega River Marina, Lamb's Yacht Center, Morningstar Marina at Mayport), slip number, and dockmaster rules.

Trailered Powerboats & Bay Skiffs

Document trailer axle condition, tires, lights, and Florida DHSMV trailer title/registration status.

Private Docks & Creek Lifts

Disclose low-tide water depth, bridge clearances (e.g., Main Street Bridge, Acosta Bridge, Ortega River Bridge), and road access.

Florida Title & Paperwork Requirements

Florida law mandates clear legal title documentation. Gather your Florida Certificate of Title (or electronic DHSMV confirmation), Florida vessel registration numbers (FL#), trailer title/registration, and any lien satisfaction forms. Documented vessels follow USCG National Vessel Documentation Center procedures. Review our boat donation paperwork checklist and IRS tax deduction guide.
