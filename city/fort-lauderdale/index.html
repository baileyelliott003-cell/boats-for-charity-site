Hundreds of miles of navigable canals feed into the New River, Port Everglades, and the Atlantic. The local fleet ranges from center consoles and offshore sportfishers to express cruisers and luxury motor yachts. But changing plans, relocations, or repowering costs often leave vessels underutilized while carrying substantial dockage costs.

Requesting an individual donation review with Boats for Charity (501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 41-2487552) allows you to close out holding costs without the friction of private showings. We review all powerboats, sailboats, yachts, and trailers individually.

Fort Lauderdale Marina Access & Canal Bridges

Canal-Front Private Docks

Disclose water depth at low tide, fixed vs drawbridge clearances along the New River or Dania Cut-Off Canal, and power availability.

Marina Slips & Boatyards

Provide marina name (e.g., Bahia Mar, Lauderdale Marine Center, Marina Mile), slip location, electrical balance, and gate access.

Trailered Powerboats

Document trailer axle condition, tires, lights, coupler, and separate trailer title status.

Florida Title & USCG Documented Yacht Transfers

Gather your Florida Certificate of Title, FL registration numbers, trailer title, and lien releases. For larger USCG-documented yachts, transfers proceed through the Coast Guard National Vessel Documentation Center. Review our yacht donation guide, paperwork checklist, and tax deduction guide.
