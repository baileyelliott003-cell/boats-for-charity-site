New River Logistics, Fixed Bridges, and Marina Moorage

Fort Lauderdale's intricate canal network presents unique vessel handling considerations. Navigating the New River requires navigating multiple bascule and railway bridges (such as the FEC Railroad Bridge and Broward Boulevard Bridge), tight channel clearances, and strict wake zones. For sportfishers, motoryachts, and express cruisers berthed upstream, captain and tender fees to move a vessel add substantial overhead to routine maintenance.

Private residential dockage along the Isles of Las Olas, Harbor Beach, and Rio Vista provides fantastic proximity to Port Everglades, but strict neighborhood association guidelines prohibit leaving neglected or inoperable vessels on dock lifts. Requesting a transparent donation review with Boats for Charity (501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 41-2487552) allows you to resolve vessel custody efficiently.

Operational Transparency: How Our Process Works

We believe donors deserve clarity at every step of the charitable process:

  • The boat remains in your possession: Your boat stays safely at your home dock, marina slip, or trailer storage space while our team manages evaluation and marketplace promotion.
  • Zero transportation costs for the charity or donor: We do not dispatch commercial haulers. Instead, we list the watercraft on established public charity auction marketplaces.
  • Buyer-arranged pickup after cleared funds: When the auction concludes and buyer funds clear, the winning buyer coordinates haul-out and transport directly with you at their expense.
  • Tax documentation delivery: You receive an official IRS Form 1098-C detailing the gross auction proceeds for your federal and state tax filings.

Fort Lauderdale Marina, Shipyard & Canal Access

When preparing your intake review, please share the specific access characteristics of your moorage:

Shipyards & Marinas

Provide facility name (e.g., Bahia Mar, Lauderdale Marine Center, Pier 66), slip number, travel-lift capacity, and vendor check-in rules.

Canal Docks & Private Lifts

Disclose canal depth at mean low water (MLW), vertical bridge clearance, seawall condition, and dock lift capacity.

Trailered Powerboats

Document trailer brake condition, tire age, Florida DHSMV trailer title/registration status, and ramp accessibility.

Florida Title & Paperwork Requirements

Florida requires clear legal ownership documentation. You will need your Florida Certificate of Title (or electronic title authorization through Florida Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles), Florida vessel registration numbers (FL#), and any relevant lien satisfactions. For USCG-documented motor yachts and sailing vessels, transfers are executed through the National Vessel Documentation Center.
