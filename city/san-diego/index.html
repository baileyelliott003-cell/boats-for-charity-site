San Diego Bay, Mission Bay Moorage, and Environmental Standards

San Diego’s saltwater environment is mild compared to tropical zones, but Pacific marine growth, constant tidal action, and copper-free bottom paint mandates require vigilant vessel maintenance. For boats berthed on Shelter Island or Glorietta Bay, monthly slip rents, electrical fees, diver hull cleanings, and mandatory liability insurance can easily reach $600 to $1,500+ every month.

Additionally, the Port of San Diego strictly enforces best management practices, prohibiting unmaintained or inoperable vessels from remaining on guest docks or long-term slips. When your sailing or offshore fishing priorities change, donating your watercraft through Boats for Charity (501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 41-2487552) allows you to end these holding expenses immediately.

Operational Transparency: How Our Process Works

We provide complete transparency throughout the charitable donation lifecycle:

  • The boat remains in your possession: You keep custody of the boat at your current San Diego slip, yard, or driveway while our team evaluates and lists the vessel.
  • Zero transportation costs for the charity or donor: We do not dispatch commercial haulers. Instead, we list the boat for public charity auction to verified buyers nationwide.
  • Buyer-arranged pickup after cleared funds: Once payment has cleared following the auction, the buyer coordinates haul-out and transport directly with you at their expense.
  • Tax documentation delivery: You receive an official IRS Form 1098-C reporting gross sales proceeds for your state and federal tax filings.

When submitting your vessel for intake review, please share the specific access characteristics of your moorage:

Shelter Island & Harbor Island Marinas

Share marina name (e.g., Kona Kai, Sunroad Resort Marina, Marina Cortez), slip number, and gate access requirements.

Mission Bay Trailered Boats

Document trailer axle condition, tires, lights, and separate California DMV trailer certificate/registration.

Mooring Balls & Anchorage

Disclose mooring field designation (e.g., A-9, Americas Cup Harbor), dinghy dock access, and tender availability.

California DMV Titling & Registration Paperwork

California requires clear documentation through the California Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV). Motorized boats and sailboats over 8 feet carry a Certificate of Ownership (California Pink Slip) and CF numbers. Trailers carry separate DMV titles or permanent trailer identification (PTI) registrations. USCG-documented yachts are handled through the National Vessel Documentation Center.
