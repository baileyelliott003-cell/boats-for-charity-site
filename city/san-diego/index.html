San Diego Marina Access & Environmental Protocols

San Diego Bay Marinas

Shelter Island, Harbor Island, Coronado, and Chula Vista slips: disclose slip account balance, key card access, and marina notice periods.

Mission Bay Marinas & Yards

Disclose bridge height limits, shallow channel draft restrictions, and travel-lift haul-out reservations.

Trailered Vessels

Document trailer tires, brake condition, lights, and California DMV trailer registration (PTI status).

California DMV Title & USCG NVDC Documentation

California requires proper title transfer through the DMV or USCG documentation for larger vessels. Gather your California Certificate of Title (pink slip), CF registration numbers, trailer PTI registration, and any lien release forms. See our paperwork checklist and tax deduction guide.
