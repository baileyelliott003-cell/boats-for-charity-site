Freshwater Chains, UV Gelcoat Exposure, and Year-Round Seasons

Unlike coastal Florida, Orlando boating takes place entirely on freshwater lakes. Without saltwater corrosion, engines and hulls often remain mechanically viable for decades. However, year-round sun exposure degrades vinyl upholstery, gelcoat, and fuel systems when boats sit parked for seasons between family outings.

When you decide it is time to part with an unused vessel, private selling often brings weeks of unverified buyers. Boats for Charity (501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 41-2487552) reviews powerboats, pontoons, ski boats, PWCs, and trailers individually with zero owner expense.

Orlando Storage Access & Trailer Logistics

Trailered Lake Boats

Driveway, side-yard, and storage facility access: document trailer tire condition, lights, coupler, and gate clearance.

Private Lake Lifts

Butler Chain, Lake Conway, and Lake Toho docks: disclose canal clearance, lift operability, and ramp access points.

Non-Running Watercraft

Disclose engine status, battery condition, fuel age, and whether the boat rolls freely on its trailer.

Florida FLHSMV Title & Registration

Florida requires title transfer through the Florida Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles (FLHSMV). Gather your Florida Certificate of Title (Form HSMV 82040), trailer registration, and lien release documentation. See our paperwork checklist and tax deduction guide.
