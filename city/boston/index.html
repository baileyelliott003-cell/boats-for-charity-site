Boston Harbor, Charles River, and New England Wintering

Boston has deep maritime roots spanning traditional downeast lobster yachts, sailing sloops, cabin cruisers, and center consoles. But operating in Massachusetts Bay requires mandatory winter haul-outs, engine block antifreeze winterization, and ice bubbler protection.

When an owner is ready to move on, private selling often requires waiting until spring while accumulating winter yard bills. Boats for Charity (501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 41-2487552) reviews vessels year-round with zero owner expense.
