New England Winter Haul-Out, Nor'easters, and Moorage Costs

Boating in Massachusetts Bay is strictly seasonal, with active cruising concentrated from Memorial Day through Indigenous Peoples' Day in October. By early November, freezing temperatures and coastal storm surges force vessel owners to haul out, drain engine cooling systems, fog cylinders, and apply protective shrink-wrap.

For vessels berthed on Boston Harbor, yearly costs for summer wet slips or mooring permits, travel-lift hauling, winter dry storage, blocking, and spring antifouling paint can easily surpass $6,000 to $12,000 annually. If you find yourself using your boat less each season, donating through Boats for Charity (501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 41-2487552) allows you to close out holding costs without out-of-pocket disposal fees.

Operational Transparency: How Our Process Works

Our guided donation framework guarantees complete transparency for every Massachusetts donor:

  • The boat remains in your possession: You do not have to move the vessel to an auction yard. It remains securely in your Boston marina slip, winter boatyard, or driveway while our team evaluates and lists the vessel.
  • Zero transportation costs for the charity or donor: We do not dispatch commercial haulers. Instead, we list the boat for public charity auction to verified buyers nationwide.
  • Buyer-arranged pickup after cleared funds: After the winning buyer completes verified payment, the buyer coordinates haul-out, rigging, or trailering directly with you at their expense.
  • Tax documentation delivery: You receive an official IRS Form 1098-C reporting gross sales proceeds for your federal and state tax filings.

Massachusetts MEP Titling & Paperwork Requirements

Massachusetts requires clear title documentation through the Massachusetts Environmental Police (MEP) Boat, ATV, and Snowmobile Registration Bureau. Motorized vessels 14 feet or longer require a Massachusetts Certificate of Title and MS registration numbers. Boat trailers are titled through the Massachusetts Registry of Motor Vehicles (RMV). Documented yachts follow USCG National Vessel Documentation Center rules. Review our boat donation paperwork checklist and IRS tax deduction guide.
