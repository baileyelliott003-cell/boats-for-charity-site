Lowcountry Tidal Ranges, Pluff Mud, and Salt Marsh Wear

Operating a boat in the Charleston area requires keen attention to dramatic semi-diurnal tides. At low water, creek depths drop dramatically, leaving hulls resting on soft pluff mud unless moored in deep-water berths along the Ashley or Cooper rivers. Unused watercraft stored on private tidal docks or mooring balls suffer accelerated gelcoat wear, blistered bottom paint, and heavy oyster bed abrasion.

For vessels berthed at Mount Pleasant or Daniel Island marinas, monthly dockage and mandatory storm evacuation contracts represent an ongoing financial liability. If your boating frequency has diminished, donating your watercraft through Boats for Charity (501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 41-2487552) allows you to close out holding costs without out-of-pocket disposal fees.

Operational Transparency: How Our Process Works

Our guided donation model ensures full clarity for South Carolina donors:

  • The boat remains in your possession: You do not need to transport the vessel to an auction yard. It remains securely in your Charleston marina slip, driveway, or private dock while our team manages the review.
  • Zero transportation costs for the charity or donor: We do not dispatch commercial haulers. Instead, we list the boat for public charity auction to verified buyers nationwide.
  • Buyer-arranged pickup after cleared funds: Once payment has cleared following the auction, the verified buyer coordinates haul-out and transport directly with you at their expense.
  • Tax documentation delivery: You receive an official IRS Form 1098-C reporting gross sales proceeds for your federal tax filing.

South Carolina DNR Titling & Paperwork Requirements

South Carolina mandates distinct titling rules administered by the South Carolina Department of Natural Resources (SCDNR). Motorized watercraft and all outboard motors over 5 horsepower must be titled separately with SCDNR. Trailers are registered through the South Carolina Department of Motor Vehicles (SCDMV). Gather your SCDNR boat title, outboard motor title, and trailer paperwork. Review our boat donation paperwork checklist and IRS tax deduction guide.
