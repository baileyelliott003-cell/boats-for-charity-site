Charleston Harbor, Ashley & Cooper Rivers, and Tidal Realities

Charleston is a historic coastal hub where the Ashley and Cooper Rivers converge to form Charleston Harbor. The local fleet features bay center consoles, dual consoles, inshore skiffs, sailing craft, and coastal cruisers. However, 5-to-6-foot tidal swings, rapid current flow, and humid summer weather demand constant maintenance.

If you have an unused or non-running vessel in the Lowcountry, private selling often takes months of coordinating tide-dependent sea trials. Boats for Charity (501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 41-2487552) reviews powerboats, sailboats, and trailers individually with zero owner expense.

Charleston Marina Access & Lowcountry Dockage

Marina Wet Slips

Charleston City Marina, Ashley Marina, Safe Harbor Charleston City, and Mount Pleasant slips: share dock location, electrical status, and marina vendor rules.

Private Creek Docks

Disclose low-tide mud clearance along Wappoo Creek, Shem Creek, or Stono River, and fixed bridge clearance.

Trailered Vessels

Document trailer axle condition, hubs, bunks, lights, and South Carolina trailer registration/titling records.

South Carolina SCDNR Title & Outboard Titling

South Carolina is unique: the South Carolina Department of Natural Resources (SCDNR) titles watercraft hulls AND outboard motors (5 HP and above) separately. Gather your SC boat title, SC outboard motor title, and trailer paperwork. See our paperwork checklist and tax deduction guide.
