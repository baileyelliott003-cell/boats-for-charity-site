Operational Transparency: How Our Process Works

Our guided donation model guarantees complete transparency for every Washington donor:

  • The boat remains in your possession: You do not need to move the boat to an impound lot. It stays safely at your marina wet slip, dry yard, or driveway while our team evaluates and lists the vessel.
  • Zero transportation costs for the charity or donor: We do not dispatch commercial haulers. Instead, we list the boat for public charity auction to verified buyers nationwide.
  • Buyer-arranged pickup after cleared funds: Once payment has cleared following the auction, the verified buyer coordinates haul-out, lock transit, or trailering directly with you at their expense.
  • Tax documentation delivery: You receive an official IRS Form 1098-C reporting gross sales proceeds for your federal tax filing.

Seattle Marina, Lock & Ramp Access Logistics

When preparing your intake review, please share the specific access characteristics of your moorage:

Marina Slips & Boatyards

Share facility name (e.g., Shilshole Bay Marina, Elliott Bay Marina, Fishermen's Terminal, Canal Marina), slip number, and liveaboard/vendor policies.

Freshwater Lake Docks

Disclose bridge clearance through Fremont/University bridges, Ballard Locks passage status, and lake depth at low water.

Trailered Powerboats

Document trailer axle condition, tires, surge brakes, lights, and Washington Department of Licensing (DOL) title/registration status.

Washington DOL Titling & Paperwork Requirements

Washington state requires compliant legal ownership transfer through the Washington Department of Licensing (DOL). Motorized vessels and sailboats 16 feet or longer require a Washington Certificate of Ownership (Title) and WN registration numbers. Manufactured boat trailers require separate vehicle titles. For USCG-documented yachts, transfers proceed under National Vessel Documentation Center rules.
