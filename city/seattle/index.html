Puget Sound Moorage, Lake Union Locks, and Winter Upkeep

Seattle is the maritime hub of the Pacific Northwest. Between saltwater cruising in Puget Sound and freshwater moorage in Lake Union and Lake Washington, thousands of vessels navigate the Hiram M. Chittenden (Ballard) Locks annually. But wet Northwest winters require continuous bilge monitoring, dehumidifiers, and canvas maintenance.

When an unused sailboat, trawler, or express cruiser sits through another rainy season, moorage and depreciation accumulate quickly. Boats for Charity (501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 41-2487552) provides an honest, no-pressure alternative to unending slip bills.

Pacific Northwest Vessel Access & Storage Context

Marina Wet Slips

Shilshole Bay, Elliott Bay, Salmon Bay, and Lake Union slips: share dock location, electrical account status, and marina vendor rules.

Freshwater Covered Moorage

Disclose height clearance, bridge restrictions on the Ship Canal, and lock transit requirements.

Dry Storage & Trailers

Note boatyard blocking status, travel-lift haul-out scheduling, or trailer roadworthiness for I-5 transport.

Washington DOL Title & USCG Documentation

Washington requires clear title transfer through the Department of Licensing (DOL) or National Vessel Documentation Center (NVDC) for federally documented vessels. Gather your Washington title (or DOL affidavit in lieu of title), WN registration numbers, and trailer paperwork. See our paperwork checklist and tax deduction guide.
