Laguna Madre Hypersalinity, Shallow Flats, and Coastal Corrosion

The Upper Laguna Madre and Baffin Bay are unique hypersaline lagoons where salinity levels often exceed the open Atlantic Ocean. While flats boats and shallow-draft tunnel hull skiffs excel in these skinny waters, the combination of extreme salt concentrations, sun, and blowing grit rapidly attacks outboard electrical harnesses, hydraulic steering lines, and galvanized trailer crossmembers.

For vessels moored at the Corpus Christi Marina or Island Moorings in Port Aransas, monthly slip rents, electrical fees, and mandatory hurricane preparedness plans continue year-round. When you are ready to retire a boat from active service, donating through Boats for Charity (501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 41-2487552) lets you resolve ownership with zero out-of-pocket disposal fees.

Operational Transparency: How Our Process Works

Our guided donation model ensures full clarity for Coastal Bend donors:

  • The boat remains in your possession: You do not need to transport the vessel to an auction yard. It remains securely in your Corpus Christi marina slip, driveway, or private dock while our team manages the review.
  • Zero transportation costs for the charity or donor: We do not dispatch commercial haulers. Instead, we list the boat for public charity auction to verified buyers nationwide.
  • Buyer-arranged pickup after cleared funds: Once payment has cleared following the auction, the verified buyer coordinates trailering and pickup directly with you at their expense.
  • Tax documentation delivery: You receive an official IRS Form 1098-C reporting gross sales proceeds for your federal tax filing.

Texas Parks & Wildlife (TPWD) Titling Rules

Texas law enforces strict dual-titling regulations. The Texas Parks and Wildlife Department (TPWD) titles motorized watercraft and all outboard motors separately. Manufactured trailers are titled through the Texas Department of Motor Vehicles (TxDMV). You will need clear Texas Certificate of Title documents for the hull, outboard motor (if applicable), and trailer, along with any lien satisfaction releases.
