Lake of the Ozarks Seasons, Freeze Risks, and Lift Docks

Boating on Lake of the Ozarks runs heavily from Memorial Day through Labor Day. When autumn arrives, owners face the annual decision to pull, winterize, and store the vessel or risk engine block freeze cracks. If a boat has sat unused on a lift for multiple seasons, accumulated storage bills make ongoing ownership impractical.

Lake Ozark Marina Access & Cove Logistics

Private Lift Docks

Disclose cove name, mile marker, dock lift operability, water depth at low pool, and road access.

Marina Slips & Boatyards

Share facility name, covered slip status, travel-lift or forklift availability, and yard vendor policies.

Trailered Runabouts & Pontoons

Document trailer axle condition, tires, bunk condition, lights, and Missouri trailer registration/title paperwork.

Missouri Department of Revenue (DOR) Titling

Missouri requires title transfer through the Missouri Department of Revenue (DOR). Motorized watercraft and outboard motors over 12 HP have separate title and registration requirements. Gather your Missouri Certificate of Title, trailer paperwork, and lien release forms. See our paperwork checklist and tax deduction guide.
