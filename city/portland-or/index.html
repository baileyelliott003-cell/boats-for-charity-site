Columbia & Willamette River Conditions, Rainwear, and Moorage

Portland watercraft operate in freshwater river systems with seasonal spring runoff, swift currents, and heavy Pacific Northwest winter rain. Bilge pumps running continuously on shore power risk battery failure, while idle hulls develop stubborn exterior moss and deck delamination.

When an owner is ready to transition away from a vessel, private selling often takes months of arranging river trials. Boats for Charity (501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 41-2487552) reviews aluminum fishing boats, cabin cruisers, sailboats, and trailers individually with zero owner expense.

Document trailer axle condition, tires, lights, coupler, and Oregon DMV trailer registration/title paperwork.

Oregon State Marine Board (OSMB) Titling

Oregon watercraft require Certificate of Title transfer through the Oregon State Marine Board (OSMB). Gather your Oregon Certificate of Title, boat registration numbers (OR numbers), trailer title, and lien release forms. See our paperwork checklist and tax deduction guide.
