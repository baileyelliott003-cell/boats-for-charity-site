Newport Harbor, Balboa Island, and Mooring Realities

Newport Beach is home to over 9,000 recreational vessels, from Duffy electric boats and Catalina sailboats to offshore center consoles and mega yachts. Between city-administered mooring fields and private marina slips along Mariner's Mile, monthly carrying costs are among the highest in North America.

When an owner is ready to move on from a vessel, private selling often requires coordinating tight harbor channel access and parking restrictions. Boats for Charity (501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 41-2487552) reviews powerboats, sailboats, and yachts individually with zero owner expense.
