San Francisco Bay Conditions, Salt Wear, and Berth Costs

San Francisco Bay is world-class water that demands continuous maintenance. Afternoon winds through the Golden Gate, strong tidal currents, and heavy marine fouling mean that even tied-up boats suffer wear on rigging, thru-hulls, and bottom paint.

When an owner decides it is time to move on, private selling on the Bay often involves months of coordinating sea trials and paying steep slip fees. SF Bay Marina Access & Harbor Logistics

Marina Berths

SF Marina (Gashouse/East Harbor), South Beach Harbor, Pier 39, and Berkeley Marina: disclose gate access, berth number, and electrical status.

Estuary & Boatyard Storage

Alameda Estuary, Richmond boatyards, and Sausalito yards: provide blocking status, travel-lift reservations, and vendor clearance rules.

Trailered Vessels

Document trailer axle condition, tires, brakes, lights, and California DMV PTI registration/title paperwork.

California DMV & USCG Documented Vessels

California watercraft require title release through the California DMV or the USCG National Vessel Documentation Center (NVDC) for federally documented yachts. Gather your California Certificate of Title (Pink Slip), trailer documentation, and lien release forms. See our paperwork checklist and tax deduction guide.
