Describe the mountain-road exit before assumptions form

A West Virginia storage address may be close on a map yet difficult for a trailer or service vehicle to reach. Photograph the driveway, grade, curves, gate, surface, overhead clearance, and space to turn; report the trailer's last road use, tire age, known brake issues, other defects, and any professional inspection. For a marina or river slip, substitute dock, ramp, current, and facility-access facts. These details help a future buyer plan; they do not mean pickup or acceptance is guaranteed.

Two West Virginia Questions to Settle Early

Can I submit a boat stored near Summersville Lake or Bluestone Lake?

You may submit it for individual review. A boat near Summersville Lake may involve different questions than one near Tygart Lake or the Ohio River. Give the exact marina, yard, mooring, residence, or storage address; describe whether the boat is afloat, blocked, lifted, or trailered; and disclose access limits, balances, liens, and facility deadlines.

Which West Virginia ownership records should I gather?

West Virginia owners should include title or registration records, trailer paperwork, lien releases, and access details for storage yards, homes, or marinas. Do not sign a title, cancel storage or insurance, or release the boat on the strength of an inquiry alone.
