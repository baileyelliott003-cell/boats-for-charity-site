How Buying a Donated Boat Works

1

Browse the current vessels listed through the official Boats for Charity eBay account.

2

Review the photographs, description, donor disclosure, location, and included items carefully.

3

Place bids and follow the auction through eBay.

4

If you are the winning bidder, follow the listing instructions for payment, paperwork, pickup, and transportation.

Each boat auction listing includes full details on vessel location, trailer availability, and pickup requirements. After cleared payment, buyers coordinate directly with the donor or facility for smooth local pickup and transportation.

Important Condition Information

Donated vessels and trailers are offered AS-IS, WHERE-IS. Boats for Charity does not perform mechanical inspections, compression tests, surveys, water tests, or trailer roadworthiness inspections.

Our listings are based on photographs and information supplied by the donor. The current condition and operation of engines, drives, electrical systems, fuel systems, steering, trailers, tires, bearings, brakes, lights, and other components have not been independently verified unless a listing specifically states otherwise.

Buyers should review every photograph and all available listing information before bidding. Pickup, transportation, registration, taxes, licensing, and applicable transfer expenses are the buyer's responsibility.

Logistics and Transportation Requirements

Because buyers are responsible for picking up vessels from the donor's facility, marina, or storage yard, make sure to plan towing capacity, roadworthy trailer equipment, and haul-out schedules. You can also explore local donation contexts in our state-by-state directory or review donor paperwork in our documentation guide.

Why These Sales Matter

Every completed, paid Boats for Charity sale helps turn an unwanted vessel into charitable funding. Boats for Charity is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, and nobody within the organization receives a salary or hourly compensation. Proceeds help us further our charitable mission and support charitable programs serving families and communities in need.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I see the boats currently available?

Our official eBay listings provide the most current inventory. Boats may be added or removed as donations arrive and auctions end.

Where are the boats located?

Donated vessels are located throughout the United States. The exact location and pickup requirements are stated in each listing.

Does every boat include a trailer?

No. A trailer is included only when the individual listing specifically states that one is included.

Does Boats for Charity inspect the boats?

No. Boats for Charity does not independently inspect, survey, water-test, or mechanically evaluate donated vessels. Listings are based on information and photographs supplied by donors.

How do I place a bid?

All bidding takes place through the official Boats for Charity eBay account. Use the links on this page to reach the current listings.
