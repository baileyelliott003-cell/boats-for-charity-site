Why Florida Boat Storage & Moorage Adds Up So Fast

Florida is the boating capital of the United States, with over 1 million registered vessels. However, tropical weather, hurricane insurance requirements, and saltwater wear create unique challenges:

  • Marina Slip Inflation: Slips in Southeast Florida and Tampa Bay routinely charge $25 to $45+ per foot per month, turning an idle 30-foot cruiser into a $12,000/year money pit.
  • Storm Season Liability: Unattended vessels risk breaking moorings during tropical storms, exposing owners to maritime salvage liens and FWC citations.
  • Engine & Manifold Corrosion: Saltwater cooling systems degrade risers and manifolds rapidly if not flushed frequently, leaving boats mechanically stalled.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I donate a boat in Florida that is sitting on a trailer?

Yes. Florida trailered boats (center consoles, bowriders, bass boats, skiffs) are among the most popular items reviewed. We will need the Florida Certificate of Title for the vessel and trailer registration.

What if the boat is currently in a Florida marina with unpaid dockage?

Please contact our team directly at (855) 557-3703. We can review the account standing and help coordinate with your marina management to resolve ownership transfer smoothly.

How much can I write off on my federal income taxes?

Under IRS Form 1098-C rules, if your boat sells at auction for more than $500, you can deduct the exact gross sale price. If it sells for $500 or less, you can claim the fair market value up to $500.
