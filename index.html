A PLAN FOR YOUR BOAT, FROM FIRST CALL TO HANDOFF

Keep the memories. Pass on the boat.

Changing how you spend your time? Downsizing, upgrading or handling an estate? You do not have to wait until your boat is a project to consider donating it.

Present your boat properly

We use the specifications, condition details, service information and photos you provide to prepare an informative auction listing. Recent maintenance and useful equipment deserve to be seen.

Let us handle buyer questions

For accepted boats, our team handles the marketing and buyer communication, checking details with you when needed. We guide the sale and payment process so you are not managing a listing alone.

Know how the handoff works

After the boat sells and payment clears, the buyer coordinates pickup with you. We help clarify access and timing and provide the applicable donation documentation, including Form 1098-C when required.

Your first conversation should leave you with a clear next step.

We review the vessel, ownership paperwork, photos, location and your timeline. Tell us about a storage deadline before committing. Review does not guarantee acceptance, a sale price or a pickup date; you remain responsible for storage, insurance and other ownership costs until transfer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it cost me anything to donate?

No. There is no charge to request a review or complete an accepted donation through us. You remain responsible for storage, moorage, insurance, security, liens, and other owner obligations until the authorized transfer is complete.

Will you take a boat that doesn’t run?

Often, yes. We review powerboats, sailboats, cruisers, yachts, PWCs, trailers, and non-running vessels case by case. When in doubt, send it in or call us.

Do I need to retitle to the nonprofit?

Not based on an initial inquiry. If your boat is accepted, our team provides step-by-step transfer instructions and coordinates local pickup and title completion seamlessly.

What deduction do I get?

You receive applicable transaction records, including Form 1098-C when required. Under IRS rules, your deduction is generally the smaller of gross sale proceeds or fair market value. Consult a qualified tax professional about your specific tax situation.

How fast is the process?

Timing varies. Ownership records, the information provided, storage and access, market interest, and the individual review all affect the process. We keep you updated along the way.

What if I still have questions?

Call us at (855) 557-3703 or send your details through the form above and a real person will reach out.
