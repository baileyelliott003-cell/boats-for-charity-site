When Your Boat Has Become a Burden

Storage costs, an estate, changing health, or years of disuse can turn a boat into an obligation. Those facts belong in the review, not behind a sales pitch.

Ready to put your boat to good use?

Share the actual condition, ownership records, storage setting, and access. A private sale may still be the better choice for a clean, marketable boat.

Done paying for a boat you no longer use?

Keep storage, moorage, insurance, and security current during review. An inquiry does not pause those obligations.

Are you ready to move on?

For inherited, co-owned, company-owned, or documented vessels, gather the authority records before anyone signs a title or release.

How It Works — 3 Clear Steps

A submission, written acceptance, sale, buyer pickup, and completed transfer are separate events.

1

Tell us about your boat

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it cost me anything to donate?

There is no charge to request a review. You remain responsible for storage, moorage, insurance, security, liens, and other owner obligations until the authorized transfer is complete.

Will you take a boat that doesn’t run?

You may submit a non-running vessel for individual review. Condition, ownership, location, storage, and access are considered together; engine status alone never guarantees acceptance.

Do I need to retitle to the nonprofit?

Do not retitle or sign anything based on an inquiry. If the boat is accepted, follow the written transfer instructions for that vessel; the owner-custody, direct-buyer-transfer model may not require an intermediate retitle before the buyer transfer.

What deduction do I get?

We provide applicable transaction records, including Form 1098‑C when required. Whether a deduction is available and its amount depend on current tax law and your circumstances; consult a qualified tax professional.

How fast is the process?

There is no fixed timeline. Ownership clarity, the quality of the information provided, storage and access, market interest, and the outcome of the individual review all affect timing.

What if I still have questions?

Call us at (855) 557‑3703 or send the known facts through the form above for an individual review.
