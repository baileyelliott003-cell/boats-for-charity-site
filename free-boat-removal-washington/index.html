Why Washington Moorage & Northwest Weather Escalates Costs

The Pacific Northwest offers world-class cruising, but seasonal weather creates serious maintenance challenges:

  • Puget Sound Moorage Rents: Marina moorage across Lake Union, Shilshole Bay, Elliott Bay, and Tacoma ranges from $15 to $35+ per foot monthly, costing owners $6,000 to $15,000 annually.
  • Rainwater & Bilge Failure: Constant Northwest winter rain causes bilge pump battery drain, cockpit pooling, and dangerous sinking risks if not checked weekly.
  • WA Clean Marina Standards: Strict environmental guidelines require containment of engine wash water and bottom paint sanding, driving up boatyard service rates.

Frequently Asked Questions

What paperwork is needed for Washington boat donation?

You will need your Washington Certificate of Title (signed by all listed owners) or WA DOL Form TD-420-040 if the title is misplaced. Read our boat donation paperwork checklist.

Does Boats for Charity accept older fiberglass sailboats or wooden boats?

We review classic fiberglass sailboats (Ericson, Catalina, Pearson, C&C, Hunter) and sound powerboats. We evaluate wooden vessels on a case-by-case basis depending on structural hull integrity.

How do I file the Washington State Report of Sale after donation?

Once we finalize the donation and issue your Form 1098-C, you can file a Seller's Report of Sale online with the Washington Department of Licensing (DOL) for a small $13.25 fee, immediately releasing all civil and financial liability.
