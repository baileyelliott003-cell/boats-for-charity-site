Two New Jersey Questions to Settle Early

Can I submit a boat stored near Barnegat Bay or Raritan Bay?

You may submit it for individual review. A boat near Barnegat Bay may involve different questions than one near the Jersey Shore or Cape May Harbor. Give the exact marina, yard, mooring, residence, or storage address; describe whether the boat is afloat, blocked, lifted, or trailered; and disclose access limits, balances, liens, and facility deadlines.

Which New Jersey ownership records should I gather?

New Jersey owners should include title and registration records, trailer title or registration, lien releases, Coast Guard documentation, and marina or yard account details. Do not sign a title, cancel storage or insurance, or release the boat on the strength of an inquiry alone.
