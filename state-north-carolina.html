Two North Carolina Questions to Settle Early

Can I submit a boat stored near the Outer Banks or Pamlico Sound?

You may submit it for individual review. A boat near the Outer Banks may involve different questions than one near Albemarle Sound or Jordan Lake. Give the exact marina, yard, mooring, residence, or storage address; describe whether the boat is afloat, blocked, lifted, or trailered; and disclose access limits, balances, liens, and facility deadlines.

Which North Carolina ownership records should I gather?

North Carolina owners should gather title and registration records, trailer paperwork, lien releases, Coast Guard documentation, and storage or marina details. Do not sign a title, cancel storage or insurance, or release the boat on the strength of an inquiry alone.
