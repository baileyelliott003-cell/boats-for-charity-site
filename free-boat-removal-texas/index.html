Why Texas Boat Moorage & Storage Costs Add Up Fast

From Galveston Bay salt air to Highland Lakes drought levels, boat ownership in Texas comes with distinct challenges:

  • Lake Level Fluctuations: Droughts along Lake Travis and Central Texas reservoirs can leave vessels stranded on inoperable boat lifts or dry docks.
  • Marina Slip Costs: Marinas in Clear Lake, Galveston, and Lake Travis charge $250 to $850+ per month for slip rental, electric hookups, and dock box fees.
  • TPWD Outboard Motor Titles: Texas requires separate title transfers for hulls and outboard motors, which often complicates private sales if paperwork is misplaced.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I donate a boat in Texas if the trailer has no license plate or expired registration?

Yes. Many stored trailers have expired Texas tags. As long as the trailer is roadworthy (or transport is arranged via flatbed), we can review the package.

What if I only have the boat title but lost the outboard motor title?

We can help evaluate your TPWD titling options or guide you through obtaining a duplicate motor title (Form PWD 143). Read our paperwork checklist.

How does the tax deduction benefit my federal return?

You can deduct the gross sale price of the boat as an itemized deduction under IRS Publication 526, turning an unwanted vessel into real tax savings.
