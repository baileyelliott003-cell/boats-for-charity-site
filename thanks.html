When will my boat be picked up?

Your boat stays at its current location while we arrange the auction. Auctions run for seven days once published; a sale and pickup date aren’t guaranteed. Pickup is coordinated afterward. If you have a storage deadline or need the boat removed by a certain date, please tell us before we list it.

What paperwork should I have ready?

Have your boat’s title or registration handy, along with any trailer paperwork. Let us know about missing documents, other owners, loans, liens, or unpaid storage fees. Please keep titles, identification, and tax documents out of the photo uploader—we’ll discuss how to provide the needed paperwork when we contact you.

Is my donation complete?

This page confirms that your information was submitted for review. It doesn’t mean your boat has been accepted or ownership has transferred. We’ll explain the arrangement and paperwork before you decide to proceed.
