Florida Junk Boat Options Compared

Florida law strictly regulates unwanted watercraft. Leaving a boat neglected can result in FWC derelict citations, municipal code violations, and costly towing liens. Here is how your options compare:

What Types of Older or Non-Running Boats Qualify?

Many boats that owners consider "junk" still possess real value in their components, engines, or hull design:

  • Trailered Outboards & Center Consoles: Yamaha, Mercury, or Evinrude powered hulls.
  • Cabin Cruisers & Sportfishers: Inboards with salvageable marine diesel or gas powerplants.
  • Project Sailboats: Sound fiberglass hulls with masts, rigging, and lead keels.
  • Personal Watercraft & Jet Skis: Sea-Doos and WaveRunners on single or double trailers.
