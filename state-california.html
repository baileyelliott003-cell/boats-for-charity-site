Two California Questions to Settle Early

Can I submit a boat stored near San Francisco Bay or San Diego Bay?

You may submit it for individual review. A boat near San Francisco Bay may involve different questions than one near Long Beach Harbor or the Sacramento-San Joaquin Delta. Give the exact marina, yard, mooring, residence, or storage address; describe whether the boat is afloat, blocked, lifted, or trailered; and disclose access limits, balances, liens, and facility deadlines.

Which California ownership records should I gather?

California owners should look for vessel title or registration, trailer title or permanent trailer identification records, lien releases, Coast Guard documentation, and marina account details. Do not sign a title, cancel storage or insurance, or release the boat on the strength of an inquiry alone.
