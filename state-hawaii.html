Two Hawaii Questions to Settle Early

Can I submit a boat stored near Honolulu Harbor or Kaneohe Bay?

You may submit it for individual review. A boat near Honolulu Harbor may involve different questions than one near Maalaea Harbor or Hilo Bay. Give the exact marina, yard, mooring, residence, or storage address; describe whether the boat is afloat, blocked, lifted, or trailered; and disclose access limits, balances, liens, and facility deadlines.

Which Hawaii ownership records should I gather?

Hawaii owners should gather registration or documentation records, trailer paperwork if there is one, lien releases, harbor account details, and any permit or storage information. Do not sign a title, cancel storage or insurance, or release the boat on the strength of an inquiry alone.
