Yacht & Sailboat Charity Donation Guide: Free Towing, Appraisals & Tax Benefits

Discover how donating a cruising sailboat, catamaran, or motor yacht to Boats for Charity eliminates expensive moorage and maintenance fees while maximizing your charitable tax deduction.

Why Donate a Yacht or Large Sailboat Instead of Selling?

Owning a large vessel entails substantial fixed monthly overhead—including marina slip fees, dry storage, bottom painting, hull insurance, and routine diesel maintenance. When an owner decides to transition away from boating, selling privately or through a brokerage often involves 6 to 18 months of ongoing carrying costs, survey contingencies, price renegotiations, and standard 10% broker commissions.

Private Sale vs. Charitable Donation

Private / Broker Sale: 6–18 months on market, continuous slip & insurance payments, 10% brokerage commission, survey haul-out costs.

Charity Donation: Immediate transfer of custody, zero future carrying costs, 100% free pickup & towing, and significant federal income tax deduction.

Tax Advantages for High-Net-Worth Donors

Donating a high-value vessel can generate substantial tax write-offs that offset federal income, business earnings, and capital gains liabilities under IRC Section 170 rules.

Key Requirements for Donating Large Vessels & Marine Assets

USCG Documentation vs. State Titling

Most yachts and large sailboats measuring 25 feet or longer and measuring at least 5 net tons are federally documented through the U.S. Coast Guard National Vessel Documentation Center (NVDC) rather than titled with a state DMV:

  • Form CG-1340 (Bill of Sale): Official Coast Guard document transferring vessel interest to Boats for Charity.
  • Certificate of Documentation (Form CG-1270): The federal registration certificate issued to the documented owner.
  • Satisfaction of Mortgage: Required from any marine lenders holding a preferred ship mortgage before title hand-off.

Marine Surveys & SAMS/NAMS Certified Appraisals

When donating a luxury vessel where the donor intends to claim Fair Market Value (FMV) under qualifying IRS exceptions (such as material improvement or program use) and the value exceeds $5,000, an independent appraisal by an accredited surveyor (Society of Accredited Marine Surveyors - SAMS, or National Association of Marine Surveyors - NAMS) is required.

White-Glove Yacht & Sailboat Donation Process at Boats for Charity

  1. Comprehensive Initial Intake: Share your vessel specifications, layout, engine hours, and marina location for our marine review team.
  2. Slip-Side Coordination & Custody Hand-Off: We work directly with your marina harbormaster to coordinate slip access, mooring releases, and transport logistics.
  3. Tax Receipting & IRS Form 1098-C: We issue full formal donation documentation and contemporaneous written acknowledgments for your tax return.

Frequently Asked Questions About Yacht & Sailboat Donations

What types of large vessels are accepted?

We evaluate motor yachts, cruising sailboats, catamarans, trawlers, sportfishers, and express cruisers in running or non-running condition.

Does Boats for Charity pay for marina slip arrears?

Outstanding marina fees and liens are evaluated on a case-by-case basis during intake. Contact us to discuss your vessel's situation.
