Donate a Commercial or Fishing Boat to Charity

Learn how commercial fishing vessels, charter boats, tugs, tenders, and workboats are evaluated for charitable donation, corporate tax deductions, and USCG documentation transfer.

Commercial Vessel Evaluation

Commercial fishing boats, dive vessels, charter craft, and utility workboats often carry substantial ongoing maintenance, slip, and commercial insurance overhead. When retiring a commercial vessel, donating to Boats for Charity (501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 41-2487552) provides a clean exit strategy and a documented tax deduction for the corporate or individual owner.

Specific Considerations for Commercial Donors

1. Corporate & LLC Ownership

Commercial vessels are often titled under an LLC or corporation. We guide you through corporate authority documentation and officer authorization.

2. Coast Guard Documentation

Commercial craft documented with the National Vessel Documentation Center (NVDC) require official USCG bills of sale and certificate transfer.

3. Commercial Engines & Gear

Document heavy diesel powerplants (Caterpillar, Cummins, Detroit Diesel), generators, winches, outriggers, and electronic arrays.

4. Mooring & Port Facility Deadlines

Disclose commercial slip agreements, port authority balances, and haul-out schedules so pickup runs smoothly.

The Transfer Process for Commercial Vessels

  1. Submit Specifications: Length, tonnage, engine specs, USCG documentation number, and location.
  2. Review & Approval: Individual evaluation by our review team.
  3. Public Sale Management: Vessel remains in owner custody until sold to a qualified buyer.
  4. Buyer-Arranged Pickup: Buyer arranges direct commercial transport, tow, or captained delivery.
  5. Corporate Tax Documentation: Receive official IRS Form 1098-C for your business accounting.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can we donate a commercial boat with equipment or permits?

Permits generally do not transfer with charitable donations, but vessel equipment, electronics, and rigging are included and documented in the public sale.

What tax records are provided for corporate write-offs?

You will receive an official IRS Form 1098-C within 30 days of sale. Review our tax-deductible boat donation guide for IRS Publication 526 guidelines.
