Boat Donation Paperwork: Complete Document Checklist

Organize boat, trailer, lien, estate, marina, transfer, acknowledgment, and IRS tax records before donation transfer.

The 6 Essential Paperwork Categories

A seamless boat donation requires clean documentation across six distinct files:

1. Boat Identity Records

Certificate of Title, state registration card, Coast Guard Certificate of Documentation, and clear photos of the Hull Identification Number (HIN).

2. Ownership Authority

Matching photo ID, estate letters testamentary, trust agreements, or corporate LLC authority documents for business vessels.

3. Lien & Balance Releases

Formal lender payoff / lien release documentation and paid-in-full marina slip records.

4. Trailer Paperwork

Trailer title certificate, transferable registration card, and VIN plate photo.

5. Transfer & Release of Liability

Signed bill of sale, state notice of transfer, and notification to your marine insurance carrier.

6. IRS Tax Records

Written acknowledgment receipt, IRS Form 1098-C (Copy B), and IRS Form 8283 filing copy.
