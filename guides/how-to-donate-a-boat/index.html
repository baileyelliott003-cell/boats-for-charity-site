How to Donate a Boat: A Step-by-Step Donor Guide

Understand boat donation from ownership checks and condition photos through review, transfer, records, and tax documentation.

The 3-Step Donation Process

1

Tell Us About Your Boat

Share your contact details, boat location, year, make, model, condition, and photos. Our volunteer team performs a rapid, no-pressure intake review.

2

We Guide the Listing & Sale

If accepted, your vessel remains safely in your custody at your dock, yard, or driveway while we prepare the listing and manage the public sale.

3

Pickup & IRS Form 1098-C

After cleared buyer payment, the buyer arranges transport directly with you, and Boats for Charity issues your official IRS Form 1098-C tax deduction receipt.
