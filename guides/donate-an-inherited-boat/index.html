Donate an Inherited Boat: Estate and Ownership Checklist

Learn what executors, heirs, trustees, and families should gather before donating a boat from an estate.

Authority Comes First

Possession is not authority. Transferring an inherited vessel requires documentation of your right to act: probate letters testamentary, letters of administration, trust certification, or small-estate affidavits. Boats for Charity reviews inherited vessels individually with zero owner expense.

Step-by-Step Estate Checklist

  1. Establish Legal Authority: Obtain certified court letters or trust documentation.
  2. Gather Ownership Records: Title, registration, bills of sale, and trailer paperwork.
  3. Align All Heirs: Secure written consent from all surviving co-heirs.
  4. Protect the Vessel: Keep insurance and dockage active until transfer is completed.
