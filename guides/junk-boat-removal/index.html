Junk Boat Removal: Safe, Lawful Alternatives & Donation Options

Before spending thousands on landfill tipping and demolition fees to get rid of an old watercraft, explore whether your boat qualifies for charitable donation review. Here is what owners need to know in 2026.

Is Your Boat Truly "Junk" or a Usable Donation?

Many owners assume a boat that has sat in storage for years is worthless. In reality, vessels often fall into distinct categories:

1. The Fixable Project

Cosmetically weathered or needing engine maintenance, but structurally sound. These vessels have strong auction demand among DIY restorers and qualify easily for donation review.

2. The Parts & Trailer Vessel

The hull may be aged, but the trailer, outboard motor, sterndrive, or marine hardware hold real market value. These are frequently accepted for charitable review.

3. The Genuinely Derelict Hull

Vessels with catastrophic structural rot, sunken water intrusion, or hazardous chemical leaks. These generally require lawful municipal or private disposal.

Lawful Pathways to Get Rid of an Old Boat

  1. Charitable Donation (Recommended First Step): Free to the donor with an IRS tax deduction receipt (IRS Form 1098-C). If accepted, the winning buyer coordinates pickup.
  2. Selling for Parts / As-Is: Listing the vessel on Craigslist or marketplace platforms explicitly as a parts vessel or project.
  3. Approved Marine Salvage Yard: Licensed boat dismantlers who recover scrap metal and dispose of fiberglass.
  4. Municipal Derelict Vessel Turn-In: Many states (like Washington DVRP or Florida VTIP) offer subsidized turn-in programs for registered vessel owners.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Boats for Charity charge to review a junk boat?

No. Our review is 100% free with no obligation. If accepted, the buyer arranges and pays for pickup after cleared payment.

Can I donate a boat without a trailer?

Yes. Many powerboats and sailboats located in marina wet slips or dry boatyards do not have trailers. We evaluate moorage access and haul-out logistics individually.
