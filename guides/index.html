Evaluating Boat Condition for Charitable Donations

Accurate condition reporting is the single most important factor in determining the viable pathways for a donated vessel. Whether a vessel is in turn-key running condition, has minor cosmetic wear, or requires major mechanical overhaul, providing objective photos of the hull, transom, engine compartment, bilge, and trailer ensures an efficient evaluation.
