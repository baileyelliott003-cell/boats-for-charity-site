Sailboat Donation Guide: How to Donate Your Sailboat

From coastal sloops to blue-water ketches and trailerable day sailers, learn how to donate a sailboat, document its rigging and sails, manage marina or mooring logistics, and receive IRS tax deduction paperwork.

Unique Factors in Sailboat Donation

Sailboats involve unique structural and mechanical systems compared to power vessels. Standing rigging, sails, keel bolts, through-hull fittings, mast stepping, and auxiliary propulsion (such as inboard Yanmar, Universal, or Perkins diesels) all factor into a vessel's review. When you donate to Boats for Charity (501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 41-2487552), we review every sailboat individually to establish an honest valuation and clear path to sale.

Key Sailboat Systems to Document

1. Rigging & Sails

Note whether the mast is stepped or unstepped, the condition of the standing wire rigging, running lines, winches, and the inventory of mainsails, jibs, genoas, or spinnakers included with the boat.

2. Auxiliary Engine & Electrics

Disclose the auxiliary power setup: inboard diesel, atomic 4 gas engine, or outboard motor bracket. Note when it was last started and any cooling or transmission notes.

3. Hull, Keel & Bilge

Describe whether the boat is fin keel, full keel, or centerboard, its haul-out history, bottom paint condition, and whether the bilge is dry.

4. Mooring, Slip, or Trailer Status

State whether the sailboat is on a mooring ball, in a private marina slip, dry-stored on jack stands in a boatyard, or on a road-legal trailer.

The Sailboat Donation Process

  1. Gather Vessel Identity: State registration, title, or U.S. Coast Guard documentation certificate.
  2. Photograph Key Areas: Cockpit, salon, mast, sails, engine, and deck hardware.
  3. Submit for Review: Our team reviews the boat's location, storage costs, and condition.
  4. Public Sale & Direct Buyer Coordination: The sailboat stays with you until sold on eBay; the buyer arranges haul-out or transport.
  5. IRS Tax Deduction: You receive an official IRS Form 1098-C documenting the gross sale proceeds for your tax return.
