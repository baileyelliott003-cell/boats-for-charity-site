Is Donating a Boat Worth It? The Real Math on Taxes, Slip Fees & Time

If you own a watercraft you no longer use regularly, you have likely weighed whether to list it for sale or donate it to charity. Here is an honest, numbers-first look at the true financial return of boat donation in 2026.

The Hidden Cost of Trying to Sell a Boat

On paper, selling a boat for cash might seem like the obvious choice. But private boat sales rarely happen overnight. In fact, according to marine industry averages, private watercraft listings typically take 6 to 12 months to sell.

During that selling period, the owner continues to pay every recurring expense:

  • Marina Slip / Moorage: $300 to $800+ per month ($1,800 to $4,800 every 6 months).
  • Dry Storage / Winterization: $600 to $1,500 annually.
  • Boat Insurance: $400 to $1,200 annually.
  • Maintenance & Battery Care: $300 to $1,000 annually.
  • Time & Stress: Answering endless messages, dealing with lowball offers, and scheduling test rides with unvetted buyers.

When Donating Makes the Most Sense

Donation is especially advantageous when any of the following apply to your situation:

  1. You are paying ongoing storage or slip fees: Every month you wait to sell drains hundreds of dollars directly from your pocket.
  2. The boat needs mechanical attention: Non-running engines, older electronics, or cosmetic wear scare off private buyers but are welcomed for charity review.
  3. You inherited the boat or are downsizing: If you are managing an estate or relocating, a structured donation avoids months of long-distance logistical headaches.
  4. You itemize deductions: Taxpayers who itemize deductions can use IRS Form 1098-C to directly reduce their federal taxable income.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get my IRS Form 1098-C?

After your boat is accepted and sold, Boats for Charity issues Form 1098-C (Contributions of Motor Vehicles, Boats, and Airplanes) within 30 days, providing the exact documentation needed for your tax return.

Do I have to transport the boat to the charity?

No. Under our guided sale process, the vessel stays safely at your location, and the winning buyer coordinates pickup directly with you after payment clears.

How does the donation review work?

You provide your vessel details (year, make, model, condition, location, and title status) online or by phone. Our team evaluates the situation and explains the next steps with zero pressure.
