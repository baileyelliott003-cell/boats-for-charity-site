How Charitable Boat Removal Works

Many boat owners search for "free boat removal" when an unused or project vessel is taking up driveway space or accumulating hundreds of dollars a month in marina slip or yard storage fees. Commercial disposal services often charge thousands of dollars for hull demolition and disposal. In contrast, Boats for Charity (501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 41-2487552) provides an alternative that costs you nothing and delivers an IRS charitable tax deduction.

The 4-Step Removal Process

1

Submit Your Vessel

Tell us the boat's location, make, model, condition, and title status. A review specialist evaluates the submission promptly.

2

Owner Keeps Custody

The boat stays safely at your slip, yard, or driveway while we prepare and manage the public listing.

3

Buyer Arranges Pickup

After the boat sells and payment clears, the buyer coordinates towing, haul-out, or pickup directly with you.

4

Receive Tax Paperwork

You receive your formal written acknowledgment and IRS Form 1098-C documenting your charitable contribution.

Common Questions About Boat Pickup

Does Boats for Charity charge any towing or administration fees?

No. Our review and donation process is completely free to the donor. There are no fees deducted from your acknowledgment.

What should I do about my marina slip fees during review?

You remain responsible for current moorage and storage until ownership officially transfers upon sale. Informing the marina early helps avoid sudden lien disputes.

What tax benefits will I receive?

You will receive an official IRS Form 1098-C reflecting the gross proceeds from the boat's sale. Read our tax-deductible boat donation guide for complete filing instructions under IRS Publication 526.
