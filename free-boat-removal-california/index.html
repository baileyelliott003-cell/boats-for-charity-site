Why California Marina & Boatyard Costs Escalate Fast

California features some of the highest coastal real estate and marina slip rates in the country:

  • Harbor Slip Rents: Marina slips across Southern California and the Bay Area range from $22 to $50+ per foot monthly, costing owners $7,000 to $18,000 annually in carrying costs.
  • Environmental HAZMAT Laws: California environmental regulations require licensed containment for engine oil, coolant, hydraulic steering fluids, and bottom paint disposal prior to landfill entry.
  • California DMV PTI Trailer Rules: California requires Permanent Trailer Identification (PTI) for trailers, and vessel title releases through the California DMV.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if I lost my California Certificate of Title (Pink Slip)?

We can help you navigate the California DMV duplicate title process (Form REG 227) or verify registration records. Read our lost boat title guide.

Does Boats for Charity accept boats that need mechanical repairs?

Yes. Many powerboats and sailboats have strong salvage, parts, or restoration value even with non-functioning inboard/outboard engines.

How does the IRS Form 1098-C tax write-off work?

When your boat sells at auction, you receive an official IRS Form 1098-C stating the exact gross sale price, which you can claim on your federal income tax return.
