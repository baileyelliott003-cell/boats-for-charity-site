Two Maine Questions to Settle Early

Can I submit a boat stored near Casco Bay or Penobscot Bay?

You may submit it for individual review. A boat near Casco Bay may involve different questions than one near Frenchman Bay or Sebago Lake. Give the exact marina, yard, mooring, residence, or storage address; describe whether the boat is afloat, blocked, lifted, or trailered; and disclose access limits, balances, liens, and facility deadlines.

Which Maine ownership records should I gather?

Maine owners should gather registration or documentation records, trailer paperwork, lien releases, mooring or yard information, and storage account details. Do not sign a title, cancel storage or insurance, or release the boat on the strength of an inquiry alone.
